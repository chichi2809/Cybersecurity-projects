## 📋 Project Overview

This project demonstrates GRC consulting methodology through a comprehensive cybersecurity assessment of HealthBridge Solutions, a healthcare SaaS company managing 1.2 million patient records and generating $45M in annual revenue.

**Project Status:** 🚧 In Progress

## 🎯 Objectives

- Assess security maturity across all 6 NIST CSF 2.0 functions
- Identify control gaps across 23 security categories
- Prioritize findings by risk level and business impact
- Develop 18-month remediation roadmap
- Enable SOC 2 Type II certification and HIPAA compliance
- Quantify ROI and business value
